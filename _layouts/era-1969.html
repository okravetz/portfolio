{%- comment -%} 1969: a teletype printout. Plain 7-bit ASCII text in 
 blocks, all capitals, no links and no markup
  inside the printout. With page.work (/1969/work/) the same printout lists every project, and a key below the
  paper (outside the printout, so it can be a link) returns to the 1969 page. The paper, its feed holes and shadow are device presentation (assets/css/era-1969.scss).
  Lines are not hard-wrapped: the 
 wraps at 72 columns (and at the screen's width on phones).
  All copy comes from _data/; _includes/era-text/ does the ASCII swaps and the capitals.
  The time-travel bar at the end is the modern exception.
{%- endcomment -%}
