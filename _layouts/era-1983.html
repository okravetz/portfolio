{%- comment -%} 1983: a bulletin board on a glass terminal. Plain ASCII text in 
 blocks, 80 columns (36 on phones),
  no box-drawing characters. The menu letters are buttons that switch sections (assets/js/bbs.js); without
  JavaScript every section is shown in turn and the menu is hidden. The beige case, the green phosphor
  glow, the scanlines and the blinking cursor are device presentation (assets/css/era-1983.scss).
  All copy comes from _data/; _includes/era-text/ does the ASCII swaps.
  The time-travel bar at the end is the modern exception.
{%- endcomment -%}
