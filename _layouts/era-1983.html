{%- comment -%} 1983: a bulletin board on a glass terminal. Plain ASCII text in 
 blocks, 80 columns (36 on phones),
  no box-drawing characters. The menu letters are buttons that switch sections, and the links (downloads,
  all work, email, LinkedIn, Back) are commands drawn the same way (_includes/era-text/cmd.html). With the
  terminal focused, typing a letter runs it (assets/js/bbs.js). Without JavaScript every section is shown in
  turn and the menu is hidden. The beige case, the green phosphor
  glow, the scanlines and the blinking cursor are device presentation (assets/css/era-1983.scss).
  All copy comes from _data/; _includes/era-text/ does the ASCII swaps.
  With page.work (/1983/work/) the screen lists every project, then a menu with one command, [B] Back (a link
  to the 1983 page), instead of the menu and sections.
  The time-travel bar at the end is the modern exception.
{%- endcomment -%}
